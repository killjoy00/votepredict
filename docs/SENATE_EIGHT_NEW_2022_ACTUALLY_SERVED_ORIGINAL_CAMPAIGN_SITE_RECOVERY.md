## Genuine original HTML recovery and date meaning

The [bounded original-source acquisition](../scripts/acquire-eight-uncovered-served-2022-senator-original-campaign-domains.ts) queries exactly eight fixed campaign domains against public Wayback original CDX **2022-01-01 to 2022-11-07** inclusive; at most 300 index rows per person and **four** original HTML replays per person (**32 maximum**). Candidate issue, policy, platform, priorities and vision URLs rank ahead of other site pages. Accept a copied original HTML page only when its actual full body matches the separately indexed Wayback original CDX **SHA-1 Base32 digest**, with original original-body SHA-256 and specific capture timestamp preserved. Dated original archived captures show candidate-owned text was publicly available **by that capture UTC**; **first author-posted date is neither necessary nor asserted**. Legacy-cycle copy (e.g. original 2020) still served on a 2022 site remains labeled old-cycle words, not newly written for a 2022 race.

A capped domain index, an HTTP error, an empty index or a source replay that does not match independent CDX bytes = **incomplete/unknown coverage**, never zero positions. This bounded attempt does not prove completeness even if all eight domains produce source pages. Additional exact short policy statements must be human selected, attributed to the individual, and reverified in a **separate exact original-byte quote audit**, with original capture date no later than election day and no inference of official Senate roll-call votes or historic forecast-feature eligibility.

The [PR-only source workflow](../.github/workflows/acquire-eight-more-actual-senators-2022-original-campaign-html.yml) archives source index queries, success/failure status, true original HTML and SHA/CDX receipts for 30 days. It does not contact public offices, access private databases, insert production evidence, train/serve forecasts, modify frozen evaluation or perform 2027/scheduled work.
