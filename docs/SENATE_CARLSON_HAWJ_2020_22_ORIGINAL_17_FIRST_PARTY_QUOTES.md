### Attribution and exact meaning

**Carlson's authentic 2020 election "Issues" page** explicitly says "PREPARED AND PAID FOR BY FRIENDS OF JIM CARLSON." Eight distinct original short site issue passages address MinnesotaCare expansion, prescription drug affordability, paid medical/family leave, clean energy and climate targets, funding public education, community infrastructure, gun violence prevention, and criminal justice reform. The original September 19, 2020 archive capture predates the November 3, 2020 election. The adjacent archived `issues1.html` page held navigation/footer but did not itself substantiate issue positions, so was **not** counted.

**Hawj's authentic October 2, 2022 candidate reelection letter** identifies itself with "Prepared and paid for by Foung for Senate 67 Committee," and says he is seeking reelection. Six short extracts explicitly list **the East Side community's priorities** (public safety, education, worker tax breaks, housing, jobs, health coverage) and must not be recast as eight independently authored formal candidate promises. Two short direct campaign sentences urge action on climate change and clean energy. The archive capture predates November 8, 2022.

**Hawj's genuine January 31, 2020 archived paid-for site** contains older-cycle text thanking voters for having reelected him to a second term, and his self-report about work on economic equity/youth summer jobs/housing. The one selected excerpt is a historical **candidate self-report**, not an independent verification of official legislative actions, nor proof this wording was newly written or adopted during 2020.

All three pages establish only an actual date **by which the exact captured wording was observable**. Original first-authored dates are unknown. No earlier availability than the archive timestamp is inferred.
