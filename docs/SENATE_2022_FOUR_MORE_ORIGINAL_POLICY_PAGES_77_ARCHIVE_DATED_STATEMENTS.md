## Original campaign source + statement proof, not a mutable page inference

The permanent [pinned original 2022 source and 77 exact policy statements](../data/evaluation/senate-four-more-2022-original-campaign-policy-excerpts-capture-dated-v1.json) include:
- All **16** individually independently obtained original 2022 HTML receipts: original campaign-hosted URL, exact historical capture UTC, original source filename, full actual HTML SHA-256, independent historical CDX SHA-1 Base32 digest, original official Senate service member identity and historical SD, and any source-cycle caveat;

- **77 short, manually selected exact source quotation substrings**, source-page/person ID, issue position family, narrow interpretation, publicly attributable-by original archive UTC, candidate-page policy context, and explicit no-individual-vote/no-forecast/autoinsertion flags.

The [offline original byte source audit](../scripts/audit-four-more-senate-2022-capture-dated-original-issue-excerpts-offline.ts) verifies official Senate serving identities against actual re-opened original government LRL source HTML SHA-256 and its independent retrieval UTC, then re-opens every one of the **16 original archived campaign HTML source bodies** from original [2022 source run #38109406776](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/actions/runs/38109406776) artifact **11690766863** (ZIP SHA-256 **59651f31286d9069d83363e7111f8490eaa98f57e598bd9d9bb211353b714195**). It verifies exact person, source URL, archive timestamp, original HTML SHA-256 and original independently indexed CDX SHA-1 against the original acquisition manifest and pinned per-page receipts, then checks every exact quote appears in the correct page's policy section and returns the real archived timestamp as source attribution date. The [PR-only real source workflow](../.github/workflows/audit-four-more-senators-77-original-capture-dated-2022-excerpts.yml) preserves a separate 30-day audited excerpt report ZIP; SHA receipts remain committed after the original 30-day source zip expires.

**Proper distinctions:** Several campaign passages discuss a senator's earlier legislative efforts or vote claims; the original text is an *authenticated first-party political/candidate claim*, not a verified official roll-call vote or factual legislative outcome. The acquisition did not prove 2020/22 SOS filed-candidate website attributes or statewide site/statement completeness. Dates here show the exact archived original website's public availability by the source capture, not original first-author date or verified earlier candidate debate/pre-primary availability. Policy quotes in this ledger are not inserted into production source/evidence rows, train a forecast, or represent an automatically admissible historic predictive feature.
