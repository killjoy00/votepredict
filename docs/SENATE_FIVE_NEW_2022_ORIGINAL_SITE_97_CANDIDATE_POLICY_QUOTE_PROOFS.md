# #864 — five more actually serving Minnesota senators: 97 campaign issue excerpts from genuine 2022 original webpages

**Evidence date rule:** a candidate-owned original archived webpage's **actual Wayback capture timestamp UTC** proves every exact issue excerpt physically present in that historic original was publicly available **by that capture**. Original first authored/written day is **not required**, is not invented, and a modern 2026 campaign page or dated publisher link is NOT proof of older original text.

The preceding [eight-person authentic 2022 first-party source acquisition, PR #1049](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/pull/1049) checked **eight independently ACTUALLY serving Minnesota Senate persons**, authenticating **23 complete original historic 2022 HTML responses from six** distinct people's campaign domains, compared against separate original Wayback CDX SHA-1 digests and full original response SHA-256. Of these, **five** have six concrete candidate-owned issue-bearing pages in the sampled originals; **Jim Carlson's four campaign page originals lack direct issue-platform text** and cannot be used to invent a position.
