The original **subdivision 1(a)** defines a principal campaign committee as *inactive* on the **later of** six years after the qualifying candidate's last election and six years after the end of the person's elective office service. This is materially different from immediate termination on losing an election or leaving the Senate. **Subdivision 2** states that the Board **may** terminate an inactive registration only after sending a written certified-mail notice and allowing **60 days**. The Board action is conditional, not a calendar-generated automatic legal termination.

Combine with the separately source-authenticated [§10A.20 and §10A.243](cfb-2023-24-primary-yearend-law-calendar-disclosure-clock-audit.md): committee reporting duties continue while required and registered, and a final voluntary termination report is an independent termination path. Do not infer any *specific* committee's registration end date from a Senate member's political tenure, report viewer references, or inactivity eligibility alone.
