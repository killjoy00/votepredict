## Why the next phase is two separate investigations

Previous [protected read-only source audit and 948-row actual media denominator](senate-media-track-d-946-987-protected-coverage-20261018.md) found **zero** previously pinned Reformer `/briefs/` and MPR `/episode/` originals in the *old 44-seed `wayback_local_trade_news` source-kind plus `local_trade_news` evidence-context filter*. That filter is not all of VotePredict. Its 805 source documents (37 Minnesota Reformer, 22 MPR) cannot establish source presence in other `source_documents.source_kind` categories, other `evidence_items` contexts, or uncatalogued ingestion systems.

The new protected [single-transaction all-stored-source-kinds audit](../../scripts/audit-senate-media-track-d-cross-ingester-readonly-v3.ts) therefore performs only:
- **One count-only denominator** over all stored `source_documents`, distinct stored source-kind categories, and all `evidence_items` rows; **never** exports individual source kinds, records, internal IDs, source URLs, source SHA, article text or person names.
- **One parameterized crosswalk** from *23 already public authenticated historical source-version fingerprints* to `source_documents.source_url`, `source_documents.metadata`, original `content_sha256`, and `evidence_items.metadata` across all stored types. Counts distinguish (a) exact archived source URL **and** full-body SHA, (b) same publisher original URL and full-body SHA, (c) URL reference only, (d) same full-body SHA without independently matched original URL, and (e) merely referenced alternate dated path. Never silently upgrades a SHA-only or URL-only observation into a proven duplicate.
- One existing, independently identified default Neon database/role, `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY`, 120-second static query cap and **explicit `ROLLBACK`**. Protected credentials are provided by the existing `evidence-readonly-sandbox` job after independently successful no-secrets tests on a reviewed `main` commit. Only aggregate numeric results indexed by already-public original capture ID leave the database. No JSONL/CSV/private article export, no production write, no new secret or branch provisioning. A nonmatch in these stored tables still does not certify absence from all external archives/publishers.

## The dry-run historical-only discovery proposal

The [shadow source and independent speaker-timing logic](../../src/evaluation/senate-media-track-d-historic-shadow.ts) is **not a source-catalog change**. It models two isolated candidate archive seed path families for historical 2021–2025 research only:

| Candidate only | Publisher original path prefix | From/through archive year | Existing production prefix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reformer | `https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/` | 2021–2025 UTC | `https://minnesotareformer.com/202` |
| MPR News | `https://www.mprnews.org/episode/` | 2021–2025 UTC | `https://www.mprnews.org/story/202` |

Historical articles without a dated slug in `/briefs/` require independently documented article year and observed original archival timestamp; the prefix **alone** must not sweep in 2026 or post-date old content. MPR dated `/episode/YYYY/MM/DD/` paths are validated *against the same exact displayed historical research date*. A 2026 URL, untrusted host, redirect, trailing query/auth, malformed date, SHA mismatch or undemonstrated archived original is rejected by the research plan. An independent observed Wayback indexed-SHA1 plus raw-original-HTML SHA256, and full captured original URL/UTC are prerequisites to an original source becoming a **verified research candidate**, not an automatically inserted evidence item.

The dry-run sample contains **23 verified archived HTML versions / 17 distinct original publisher URLs** from *earlier, already merged public original proofs*, not a fresh publisher scan and **not 23 distinct news articles**:

- [#946](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/issues/946): original **12** captures / **10** Reformer `/briefs/` URLs from the previous protected audit, plus **five additional authentic captures / three more original `/briefs/` URLs** from the [2021 commissioner, 2022 special session and 2025 paid-sick-leave original recovery](senate-media-three-original-reformer-brief-historical-recovery-receipts-20261011.json): **17 versions / 13 original brief URLs** across all five publisher-displayed study years.
- [#987](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/issues/987): **six** previously verified MPR HTML archive versions across four original pages (five version snapshots under `/episode/`, one `/story/` Benson control). The proposed new `/episode/` prefix intentionally does **not** include the existing `/story/` control. Previously unverified 2022 Benson/Kunesh and 2025 Pha/Coleman original pages are **not** upgraded merely because their publisher URLs exist today.

The shadow selection output compares **actual currently configured seed prefix + topical URL score** to new research-only candidates, and lists public capture IDs and metadata-only dispositions. It does **not** change `LOCAL_TRADE_NEWS_SEEDS`, change the topical-priority gate, invoke Wayback in the dry run, run a scheduler, write DB records, deduce 2021–25 publication recall or mark content as eligible for a retrospective forecast. If a subsequent *separately approved* historical capture design is activated, it must additionally use exact bounded CDX, respect robots/response limits and source retention rules, recover original raw bytes before SHA/publisher equivalence assertions, avoid member-vote inferred stances, and deduplicate **only with authenticated historical two-sided source proof**.
