## What the distinction actually means

- **Individual direct attributed quote on a caucus page (14 pages):** The caucus site currently represents those words as a named senator's statement. This is stronger *publisher attribution* than an unquoted mention, but not independently authenticated historical original bytes or original spoken audio.
- **Original first-party MPR interview page (1 page):** The Nov 15, 2021 MPR story directly quotes Sen. Jeremy Miller and links interview audio, resolving the original URL. The quote is presently in the publisher article; historic HTML/audio body/version and independently verified time remain outstanding. Do not automatically transfer a different Miller excerpt second-hand cited by Marty.
- **Original third-party Q&A (1 page):** The *Enterprise Minnesota* page labels the questioner and Jeremy Miller's answers in alternating turns. It is a new direct-answer research lead, **not** an authenticated audio tape and **not** the separately referenced November 15 MPR interview.
- **Collective statement (2 pages):** The collective text cannot be transformed into separate individual senator quotes. In 2025 the quoted signatories even span the **Senate and House**. Member rosters and bylines do not overcome the attribution limit.
- **Contextual senator names:** Co-authors, interview subjects cited by another speaker, deceased predecessors, and named political opponents remain context-only unless their distinct words in the same source can be separately established and attributed.
