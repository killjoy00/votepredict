## Boundaries

Original historical printed reporter quote snippets are *not* source audio, a reporter's complete context-independent utterance transcript, a human-approved quote, original authoring clock, a committee vote ledger, or predictive training features. If the original raw body is unavailable or has a differing SHA, its phrase status must remain **UNKNOWN**, never “negative.” Unverified newspaper-reported voting assertions are separately labeled and never imported as authenticated recorded member votes. These 15 selected URLs are not a complete 2021–25 newsroom/senator/year publication population, and speaker-turn candidates are not unique statewide senator counts.
