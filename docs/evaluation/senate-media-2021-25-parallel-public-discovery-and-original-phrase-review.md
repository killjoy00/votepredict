## Track B — specific historical original-HTML short-phrase review

Before interpreting even a short piece of present-day publisher text as **historical** a constrained public-only script first authenticates the exact **original archived response bytes** again. The old 18-source sample yielded [24 indexed-SHA1-matching original HTTP capture versions across 13 URLs](senate-media-2021-25-new-public-original-replay-receipts-20261011.json); those are **24 archived body versions, NOT 24 individuals, speech acts or approved quotations**.

The new [seven-phrase review queue](senate-media-2021-25-pinned-historical-original-phrase-review-queue.json) deliberately covers **six distinct previously SHA-pinned original captured HTTP bodies**:

- **Nov 15, 2021 MPR original Jeremy Miller interview:** check a direct reporter-attributed Miller text fragment and **separately** a phrase only claimed in John Marty's Nov 16 contextual rendering of the interview. Even if that latter phrase is absent from historic MPR page HTML, this says NOTHING about whether it was in original recorded audio.
- **Nov 16, 2021 John Marty DFL first-person release:** check one Marty quotation inside the SHA-pinned original Nov 16 page, never count its Miller allusion as John Marty's quotation.
- **Mar 2021 Julia Coleman caucus release:** check short Coleman-attributed original visible text; David Senjem's contextual bill-author mention is not a quote from Senjem.
- **Jan 24 / Jan 26, 2024 John Marty online sports betting release:** check short original HTML wording in the first **March 2** archived version; the capture DOES NOT prove which of the two current-page date labels was original publication time.
- **Apr 3, 2025 Karin Housley bonding release:** check archived April 5 version, without inferring floor votes.
- **2023 Senate POCI collective statement:** check only the collective text in the separately independently authenticated June 5 original version, never assign it to any listed individual senator.

The [original phrase-check implementation](../../src/evidence/senate-media-historical-phrase-presence.ts) re-downloads only these **six exact original Wayback id_ URLs**, revalidates **CDX indexed SHA-1**, **previously pinned original HTTP body SHA-256**, and **original body byte count** before checking a short normalized HTML-visible text cue. HTML head, embedded scripts, styles, template, SVG, comments, attributes and article audio are **not treated as quote text**. Redirect, original HTTP error, compressed or changed body, too-large response, text not found, and byte or digest mismatches all fail closed. **A positive visible-markup fragment is a useful research cue, not an approved senator quote or historical audio proof.**

Run on [the restricted public research workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-media-historical-original-phrases.yml) or within a checkout:

    node --import tsx scripts/audit-senate-media-historical-original-phrases.ts --output artifacts/senate-media-historical-original-phrase-cues.json

The workflow runs on the exact owner-branch PR or manual main dispatch, without private credentials. It writes **metadata-only** seven cue statuses to a seven-day artifact; **no archived HTML, original speech audio or full quotation passages persist**.

### Evidence classes and what they establish

| Observation | What it can establish | What it **cannot** establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current publisher page date and named quote | An original source candidate and visible publisher attribution today | Exactly what historical page said, first publication time, captured audio |
| Indexed CDX candidate | A selected URL and candidate archive UTC / indexed digest | Original HTTP body retrievable or unchanged |
| Exact indexed-SHA1 **and** previously pinned raw SHA256 matching original response | That exact HTTP body version existed by its capture UTC | First original publisher posting clock or who originally spoke what |
| Text cue found in approximate original visible HTML | Short bytes-normalized phrase appeared in that verified archived page's HTML text | Distinct speaker-turn boundary, human attribution approval, original audio |
| No phrase cue found in verified visible HTML | That short normalized string was **not found** by this text approximation in **that original archived page body version** | Audio absence, all paraphrases/other versions absent, no historic interview |
| Current page contains a named senator **only contextually** | A lead to look for that speaker in an independent original | A directly attributed quote from the named senator |
| Collective Senate caucus or mixed-chamber statement | One collective publication by a named group | Separate quotations attributed to each participant or to House members as senators |
