# #864 report-version scope — recovering the previously omitted 2022 SD1–12 original PDFs (V15)

The existing [official CFB 2022 Senate SD1–12 original pre-primary and pre-general report PDF proof](source-proof/cfb-2022-senate-24-original-ce-pdf-headers.psv) authenticates **24 exact original candidate committee PDFs**, 12 registration identities, reporting periods, official Board receipts and original 2022 disclosure-calendar release lower bounds. The source authentication is original and complete, but **not all earlier candidate source batches were required to prove their committee principal personally served in the Minnesota Senate**. The newer [source-proven actual 95-serving-senator year-overlap inventory](source-proof/cfb-2021-25-95-serving-senator-report-version-source-crosswalk-v14.psv) did not yet include those 24 PDFs.

This continuation does **not** acquire or infer a legally owed/received Senate annual-report or filing-obligation denominator. The only question: do the actual official CFB 2022 district name/registration labels for these **12 exact registration IDs** independently match people in the source-proven actual-serving Senate 2021–25 roster for the relevant district in either 2022 or 2023? That permits already-authenticated original CFB PDF body/source metadata to enter the senator report-*version* inventory while never admitting never-serving candidates, ambiguous matches, unrelated source transactions, or retrospective public dates.

## Source boundaries

- Original already independent [24 full authentic official CFB PDF body SHA256/bytes and first-page identity receipts](source-proof/cfb-2022-senate-24-original-ce-pdf-headers.psv), backed by [original 2022 24-PDF source ZIP/calendar checkpoint](source-proof/cfb-2022-senate-24-original-ce-pdf-source-checkpoint.json). Any source drift is a hard HOLD.
- Independently reacquired and indexed complete [95 actual-serving original LRL person/term-year roster V14](cfb-2021-25-95-served-senator-report-version-panel-crosswalk-v14.md), supported by original LRL Senate directory and special-senator swearing HTML full source body fingerprints.
- Official CFB 2022 candidate district selection pages **Senate SD1–12**, same 2022 election viewer segment as pinned 24 C/E original report version sources. The [source acquisition script](../../scripts/audit-cfb-864-2022-first-12-original-pdfs-v15.mjs) re-fetches each one, bounds and SHA256s its original entire government HTML response, reads exact source regulator registration number and candidate display label from the source viewer, and matches to a *unique independently established actual-serving Senate original LRL identity* with a matching 2022 or 2023 Senate district. A name alone or a winning election is not enough.
- Each authorized positive returns exact official CFB registered source principal, independently proved senator, two actual source-authenticated original 2022 C/E PDF version IDs and complete original CFB PDF SHA256/bytes, actual source report period, Board receipt, calendar deadline, and possible release floor. Historical exact PDF public-by remains **UNKNOWN**, not date of Board receipt or source acquisition.

The [PR-only public original source job](../../.github/workflows/senate-864-2022-first-12-original-pdf-serving-audit-v15.yml) prints metadata-only result. It does not print the identity of unrelated/non-serving CFB candidates; does not output financial transactions, PDF pages/bytes, names/addresses of donors, private database rows; does not contact or schedule anyone. HTTP failures or CFB label drift mean source HOLD, not evidence absence.

## Goal and honest stopping condition

When each exact 2022 candidate registration maps to an independently serving person, **append only verified 24 PDF version identities** to the [existing 229 source version ledger](cfb-2021-25-served-report-version-unified-v12.md) and then regenerate the 95-person existing-CFB source report-panel crosswalk. Keep other 70/unknown Senate people explicitly unknown until an original CFB report source join is independently recovered. Existing [two original Erin Murphy 2021 and 2022 year-end PDFs](source-proof/cfb-2021-22-senate-one-filer-original-reports-and-calendars.json) can be merged separately with first-page and original source body SHA proof, because she is an independently verified actual-serving Minnesota senator.

This is a **report-version evidence completeness** project, *not* a research exercise in the statewide legal obligations of every person or committee. User excluded bulk original finance CSV parity, older evidence eligibility and legal filing-duty counts; none of those are necessary here. Source version exact historical public-by remains unproved until an independently contemporary full original PDF binary hash/timestamp meets the individual original report's legal lower bound.
