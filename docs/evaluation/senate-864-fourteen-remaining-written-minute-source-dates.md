# #864 — exact 14 official 2022–2025 Minnesota Senate written-Minutes gaps

**Scope:** 2022–2025 historically indexed Senate committee dates **without authenticated written committee Minutes after every existing original source recovery**. These are source-coverage holds, not recorded vote counts or proof that no motions occurred.

## Independently reconciled denominator

The original SHA-pinned Legislative Reference Library electronic committee index has **1,592** 2022–2025 committee/day entries. The 141 original no-linked-PDF dates were reconciled against **111 government Clerk HTML written Minutes** and one independent archived original LRL Housing PDF, leaving **29** unverified dates (1,563 written-minute days total). Two further original-LRL public source studies independently authenticated **4 meeting days in 8 original PDF files** and **11 more meeting days in 11 original PDF files**. Therefore **1,578/1,592** indexed meeting dates have authenticated written Minutes and **14** still do not.

## 2021 print and 2022 printed/electronic provenance boundary

Minnesota's [official Senate Minutes catalog](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/minutes/) identifies the 1999–2022 print collection and warns the **2022 printed and electronically published sets may differ**. Authenticated full 2021 official printed Minutes and complete 2022 printed-to-electronic source comparison **remain unavailable from this project's verified publicly acquired originals**. The official electronic archive is therefore **not** a complete official 2021–2025 recorded-motion denominator. The hearing-day event/public-by convention selected by the owner is not proof of an independently known PDF first-publication timestamp.

These 14 dates are the exact queue for independent, non-contact follow-up via self-service original authenticated government Minutes, archived committee/journal records or *exact* attributable playable committee recordings. An index entry, generic media player, other committee's clip or text cue alone cannot certify a motion, speaker, member YEA/NAY or zero-action meeting. Do **not** contact the Legislature, LRL or public offices. Do not infer individual votes from attendance, aggregated counts, voice votes or an unclear speaker.

**No Neon reads/writes, old positional event-key replay, model retraining, forecast serving changes or historical individual votes were produced by this gate.** Issue #864 remains open pending actual original source-to-stored motion/member parity and an independently verified complete documentary denominator.
