# Public source expansion program

Tracking: issue #355.

## Goal

Expand VotePredict's durable public-information corpus across eight source families while preserving the same strict event-time rule used by lifecycle research: a historical model may use an item only when an independent source proves it was public before the model cutoff.

The expansion does not change serving probabilities. All new evidence defaults to context / non-mechanical until separately evaluated.

## Frozen availability hierarchy

1. **Official publication timestamp** — dated government/legislative source intended as a historical record.
2. **Independent archive capture** — exact Internet Archive capture timestamp; the capture timestamp is the availability bound.
3. **Regulatory disclosure/publication date** — an official regulator date showing when the report, notice, or record was public is sufficient. An exact filing timestamp is not required when that disclosure date is directly established. A proven filing date plus a documented publication-delay rule may also establish availability. Transaction/event dates alone do not qualify.
4. **Publisher page metadata** — accepted for verified publisher/member-primary pages when the publication timestamp is internally carried by the source.

For date-granular lifecycle replay, evidence must satisfy `available_date < cutoff_date`. Same-day material is excluded unless the historical source proves ordering precisely enough for the relevant model.

### Conservative availability upper bounds

The historical contract does **not** require recovery of the exact first publication instant. A later independent observation may establish a conservative **available-by** bound when it proves that the exact source/report/row was already public by that date. In that case, replay may use the item only for cutoffs strictly after the proven bound. This intentionally delays evidence relative to its unknown true publication time and therefore remains leakage-safe.

Examples of acceptable upper-bound proof include an exact Wayback capture of the regulator report/viewer page, an official regulator record showing the exact report was filed/published by a specific date, or another immutable official artifact that demonstrably contains the exact row. Reporting-period end dates, transaction/activity dates, statutory filing deadlines, election dates, and assumptions that a filer complied on time do **not** establish an available-by bound by themselves. Metadata should distinguish an exact/derived publication date from a conservative upper bound rather than silently presenting the bound as the original publication timestamp.

## Eight families

1. **Wayback campaign sites.** Backfill SOS-filed campaign home/issues/news/about pages using CDX capture timestamps and preserve original URL, archive URL, capture time, digest, fetched content hash, and supersession.
2. **Independent expenditures.** Preserve candidate, spender, amount, support/oppose designation where reported, entity type, reporting source, and disclosure availability. The transaction date remains event provenance only. A row becomes historically eligible from an official CFB disclosure/publication date that applies to that row, or from a report-level availability date when the row is demonstrably contained in that report. Same-day use remains excluded for date-granular replay. Money remains context and never becomes an inferred legislative stance.
3. **Finance disclosure timing.** Reconstruct report/notice availability from official CFB reports, disclosure listings, historical calendars, filed-report records, and large-contribution notices. The operative historical date is when the information became publicly disclosed, not when the underlying transaction occurred. A direct official disclosure/publication date is sufficient even when the exact filing timestamp is unavailable. When only a filing date is proven, a separately documented regulator publication-delay rule may derive `availableOn` (for electronically filed campaign-finance reports, the existing CFB rule remains `availableOn = filedOn + 1 day`). Contribution, receipt, expenditure, reporting-period, and statutory due dates remain provenance only unless the official source explicitly identifies that date as the public disclosure date. A report-level availability date may be applied only to records demonstrably contained in that report; mixed-period/cycle aggregates remain historically ineligible unless they are partitioned or mapped to their component disclosure dates. Regulatory disclosure proofs must retain an official `cfb.mn.gov` HTTPS provenance URL; third-party pages cannot establish CFB disclosure timing.
4. **Endorsements/questionnaires/scorecards.** Preserve organization, candidate/member, question/response or endorsement record, publication/archive date, source hash, and neutral provenance. Do not infer legislative votes from an endorsement.
5. **Lobbying subjects/associations.** Preserve association/principal, lobbyist, specific subject/category, spend/report period, disclosure/publication date, and source. Do not infer member stance from lobbying relationships. The Board's July 10, 2024 minutes identify the January-May 2024 lobbyist activity report as the first report to disclose specific lobbying subjects, so the program must not fabricate pre-2024 specific-subject history. For subject/category evidence, report periods, activity dates, statutory due dates, and principal expenditure years are provenance only. Historical eligibility begins on a directly proven official disclosure/publication date, or on another independently proven availability date for the specific report. Exact filing time is not required when the disclosure date itself is proven. The campaign-finance electronic-report next-day publication rule is not assumed for lobbyist reports.
6. **Committee/hearing material.** Ingest agendas, schedules, testifier lists, written testimony/handouts, amendments, minutes, roll calls, bill summaries, and fiscal-note revisions with official dates and bill resolution. The archive metadata collector preserves the official posted date and attachment URL first. The current-PDF collector fetches only those already-indexed official House PDF attachments in bounded batches, preserves exact byte SHA-256/fetch timestamp/archive-page lineage, and keeps those current bytes `asOfEligible=false`: the archive posted date proves the historical listing/link, not that bytes fetched today are identical to the historical bytes. A separate Wayback-PDF collector may establish historical content identity only by fetching an actual archived PDF capture of the exact attachment URL; for those archived bytes, the exact Wayback capture timestamp is the availability bound and the capture digest, archived-byte SHA-256, fetch timestamp, original URL, archive URL, and parent archive-page provenance are preserved. Document/event dates found inside a PDF never substitute for availability. All attachment-body evidence remains neutral context with `modelWeight=0`, `mechanicallyActionable=false`, and same-day ineligibility.
7. **Member/caucus archives.** Backfill House/Senate/caucus publications natively where historical dates survive, otherwise require pre-cutoff archive captures. House uses its official LRL-keyed member news archive and its explicit archive dates. Senate uses the existing verified DFL/Republican caucus profile/article discovery rules; a historical Senate article is accepted only when the caucus page still verifies the target member, carries an internal publisher publication timestamp/date, and that date maps unambiguously to one active 2021-26 Senate membership. Undated, identity-ambiguous, out-of-membership, or future-dated pages fail closed. Publisher dates establish availability only under the publisher-page-metadata rule; same-day use remains excluded, and all member/caucus material remains neutral/non-mechanical with `modelWeight=0`.
8. **Targeted local/trade news.** Expand curated publisher discovery around Minnesota legislators/bills/issues; historical use requires publisher metadata or a pre-cutoff archive capture. The v3 archive universe contains **44 bounded publisher prefixes** spanning statewide/statehouse outlets, television/public-radio newsrooms, regional newspapers, community publications, business/trade press, student media, public-affairs television, radio networks, and culturally focused Minnesota news. The original statewide/regional set plus the first broadening tranche includes Star Tribune, Axios Twin Cities, CBS Minnesota, KSTP, FOX 9, Duluth News Tribune, Minnesota Lawyer, MPR News, Minnesota Reformer, MinnPost, Finance & Commerce, Mankato Free Press, Post Bulletin, Agweek, Brainerd Dispatch, Bring Me The News, Bemidji Pioneer, West Central Tribune, Pioneer Press, KARE 11, Sahan Journal, Minneapolis/St. Paul Business Journal, Minnesota Spokesman-Recorder, Mesabi Tribune, Marshall Independent, Winona Daily News, St. Cloud Times, Twin Cities Business, KAXE, Sun Thisweek/HometownSource, Republican Eagle, and Northern News Now. The second breadth tranche adds **The Minnesota Daily, Minnesota News Network, KNSI, Twin Cities PBS / Almanac at the Capitol, InForum / Forum News Service, Grand Forks Herald / Forum News Service, Mshale, The UpTake, North News, Roseau Times-Region, Mille Lacs Messenger, and Isanti-Chisago County Star**. Per-source path hints allow narrow, high-value archives whose URL structure is not itself obviously legislative while preserving the same fetched-page verification. Archive selection reviews up to **40 candidate captures per source** in the final deep follow-up pass (the collector hard ceiling), up from 24 in the breadth pass. The extraction identity remains `local-trade-news-history-v3` because the verification/extraction semantics did not change. A fetched snapshot is retained only when it has explicit Minnesota legislative/statehouse language, an HF/SF identifier, **or an unambiguous recognizable name match to a 2021-26 Minnesota legislator**. That member-name path is important for district/local reporting that discusses a legislator without literally writing “Minnesota Legislature.” For archived pages, the exact Wayback capture timestamp is the availability bound; publisher dates are preserved separately as provenance and never substitute for archive availability. These records remain neutral context with `modelWeight=0`, `sameDayEligible=false`, and no inferred member or bill stance.


### Deep follow-up press review

After the completed breadth review reached 410 historically timed items, a final depth pass re-runs the same 44-source universe at the collector's existing 40-capture hard ceiling rather than adding more publishers. The purpose is to inspect lower-ranked but still topically targeted archive pages that the 24-capture pass did not reach. **This is a deeper selection pass, not a new extraction version**: the collector keeps `local-trade-news-history-v3` so pages already persisted under identical verification semantics reuse their existing ingestion identity and only genuinely new verified articles increase the corpus. Historical availability, same-day exclusion, member/bill verification, neutral stance, and zero model weight are unchanged.

### Broad press review result

The completed 44-source broad press review materially expanded the historical local/trade-news corpus without changing evidence semantics.

- Final post-retry read-only audit: workflow `37090752375`.
- Historically timed local/trade-news items: **410**, up from the pre-review **112** (**+298**).
- Session totals: **138** in 2021-22, **113** in 2023-24, and **159** in 2025-26.
- First breadth tranche net additions: **136** across workflows `37087156349`, `37087166820`, `37087559584`, and `37089446884`.
- Second breadth tranche net additions: **160** across workflows `37088990172` and `37089252998`.
- The 10-source second-tranche batch selected 216 captures, fetched 214, accepted/inserted 134, and recorded only two snapshot 404 failures; 93 accepted pages contained direct recognizable legislator-name matches.
- The final eight-source first-tranche cleanup selected 136 captures, fetched 133, accepted/inserted 74, and recorded three transient Republican Eagle fetch failures.
- A final bounded transient-failure retry (workflow `37090580654`) rechecked KNSI, Almanac at the Capitol, and Republican Eagle, reused 32 already-persisted items, recovered **2 additional articles**, and left only two fail-closed archive fetches (one Almanac 404 and one Republican Eagle fetch failure).
- The first broadened live production refresh after deployment (workflow `37087362760`) processed 24 memberships and inserted **4 verified publisher-news articles** plus **38 member-primary publications**. GDELT timeout/rate-limit warnings fell back to Bing News RSS and did not change serving behavior.

The corpus remains context-only/non-mechanical. Archive availability is still the exact Wayback capture timestamp, same-day use remains excluded, and no article is converted into a member or bill stance merely because it names a legislator or discusses legislation. No serving/model change was made.

## Modeling boundary

The source expansion may support new research feature matrices, but retrospective 2021-26 results remain development/robustness evidence. Governing confirmation stays prospective. No automatic model promotion or serving change is allowed.
