# Independent Secretary of State 2023 Senate roster verification for 22 actual finance-research senators

**Original public-source evidence for finance track #864; 2026-10-11**. This source research independently verifies **the 2023 Minnesota State Senate roster identity** for all 22 senators in the preexisting 2022-verified CFB year-end finance sample. It does not change the 71-version finance-report availability audit and does not assess anyone outside the existing identified people.

The Minnesota Secretary of State's official [Minnesota Legislative Manual 2023–2024 Pocket Edition](https://www.sos.mn.gov/media/ybhkokxo/pocket-edition-2023.pdf) contains a **67-member Senate roster** in the section headed *MEMBERS OF THE STATE SENATE*, with each member printed by name and district. The original Secretary of State PDF was independently retrieved and parsed from the government host; **5,803,052 bytes**, SHA-256 `0a7fe8ff6b602dcc8c0d1b00e274f3f2d883f651c3666669c16eb7509731ef03`. The Board finance history's prior 71-viewer-reference roster remains independently pinned at Git blob `bfa1c1eff6a095a3a0d03aa49a34e1d929d6a388`.

**22 / 22 independently match by exact family name, given name and Senate district**, and there are **0 unmatched, duplicate or contradictory** 2023 Senate roster identities. This is an important upgrade from presuming that someone serving in 2022 must have been serving in 2023. One exact human-name normalization, *Bruce Douglas Anderson* versus printed *Anderson, Bruce D.*, is supported by the same first name, surname and actual district 29 rather than being silently assumed from full-name equality.

**Temporal and legal limits remain important:**

- The original 2023 roster is **not proof that a named senator served at an arbitrary day of 2023**, or remained an active state senator throughout 2024. A separate 2024 original Senate roster, time-specific journal or official membership event audit is required for 2024 claims.
- The roster does **not** prove that a CFB principal committee was actively registered or not terminated, or whether that committee **legally owed** any specific 2023/2024 financial report. The expected/due counts in the Board's other statewide memos are mixed-filer aggregates, not candidate-by-candidate legal filing obligations.
- **None** of our 71 CFB 2023/2024 year-end version references has independent actual public-by evidence or vote-day prediction eligibility yet. Senate membership proof does not cure this historical evidence deficiency.
- The original Secretary of State PDF is parsed in memory only. The ledger stores only authenticated senator names, districts, committee registration and source fingerprints. No raw PDF, full text, addresses, office locations, phone numbers, donor identities or transaction data were persisted.

No production database, forecast, machine learning, serving, scheduler or 2027 scope was altered. Keep [issue #864](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/issues/864) **OPEN**.
