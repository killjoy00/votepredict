# Senate #864 — last three candidate-owned historical site gaps, independently original-audited

**Scope:** Three senators who actually served during 2021–2025 but still lack an independently authenticated original **candidate-owned Minnesota Senate election campaign website**: **Charles W. Wiger (SD43), David J. Tomassoni (SD6), Thomas M. Bakk (SD3)**. Each was re-joined to the full original MN LRL actual Senate service, independent 2020 Secretary of State 67-district general election winners, and prior all-95 source matrix. Probing covers 2007–2020 historical archives, but an original capture from 2008 does **not** count as a 2020 authored policy.

## Key source outcome

**Zero new candidate-owned Senate sites verified.** The source program found *genuine* archived HTML for two historical campaign-domain hypotheses, but proved these particular original pages are **not** Senate campaign platforms. Keeping negative source evidence distinct from absence is essential.

## Cumulative research coverage and evidentiary separation

**Unchanged: 95** actually served Minnesota state senators during 2021–2025, **92/95** with previously separately authenticated candidate-owned original historical election sites, **1,285** short selected original candidate-site issue fragments. These candidate website people remain unobserved: **Wiger, Tomassoni, Bakk**. Older authentic *publisher direct candidate Q&A*, genuine published caucus interviews, actual official Senator press statements and authentic government member pages exist and can support **separately typed** research, but cannot be added to candidate-owned-site site counts.

An archive provider's HTTP 200 non-CDX failure or lack of an indexed URL is **unknown**, not a finding that a candidate had no campaign site, no policy or did not campaign. An archive capture date is only the date content was observable, not proof it was first authored then. Campaign statements regarding legislative actions do **not** independently verify official Senate floor/committee votes.

The companion Secretary of State historical filed-candidate website-field investigation is **PR #1237**; if it obtains original filed campaign domains, run another truly targeted whole-historical-website-original audit rather than extrapolating from guessed domain names.
