## Actual historical public-by: **0/229 authenticated**

The dates recorded as Board receipts and statutory legal nonpublicity floors are explicitly NOT timestamps proving that a specific report version was publicly accessible then. The authenticated government PDF original may have been fetched in 2026 even though its financial coverage concerns 2021–2025.

The exact historical-public-by field remains blank/UNKNOWN for all 229 official version references. This is **not** a claim that the PDFs were never posted. A future positive requires a contemporaneously timestamped display/distribution of the **exact original PDF binary**, source URL and registration/year/period/amendment version, independent original hash agreement, and capture time later than the statutory gate. A Board memo mentioning a version is corroboration of Board possession, not public distribution of the exact binary. The earlier [four-version exact Wayback 2023/24 CDX pilot](cfb-2023-24-exact-original-pdf-version-archive-publicby-research.md) returned four verified empty responses for those queried URLs only, not exhaustive archive absence.
