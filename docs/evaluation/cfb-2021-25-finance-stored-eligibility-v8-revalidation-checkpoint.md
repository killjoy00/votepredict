## Newly closed source-code false-positive paths (v8)

1. `cfbElectronicReportAvailableOn(filedOn,dueOn)` remains a **conservative legal not-before** calculation, including late-filed reports. Minnesota [§10A.20 subd. 1b](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/10A.20) does not independently certify that an original or amended PDF body was historically publicly accessible on this date.
2. `buildCfbReportDisclosureProof` and `parseCfbReportPdfAvailability` keep their backward-compatible returned filing metadata / earliest floor but clearly label `proofKind=cfb_report_filing`. `firstCfbReportAvailabilityForTransaction` now refuses **all** filing-derived windows regardless of apparent dates or present-day source PDF row containment. Directly dated official-disclosure windows still require correct source/coverage and may not claim public availability before a known statutory floor.
3. The independent-expenditure bulk backfill no longer creates or promotes `DisclosureMapping` entries solely from `filedOn + dueOn`. Its promotion SQL is restricted to separately source-labelled direct-disclosure mappings, and rows with only filing metadata are preserved as unpromoted context.
4. `CFB_REPORT_AVAILABILITY_VERSION` advances to `mn-cfb-report-availability-v8`, and the **offline historical debt auditor** flags stored earlier versions, including v7, for revalidation even when their date equals the legal floor. No previous persisted `asOfEligible`, `availableOn`, `published_at`, associated reports or feature totals were rewritten.

The existing CFB report PDF parser's `window.availableOn` legacy field is a **floor** for `cfb_report_filing`, not a historical public-by certificate. It remains useful for negative temporal checks and source review, not for positive replay features. Source-text co-occurrence with a date/amount/contributor is only a heuristic containment lead until authenticated original-version transaction identity is established.
