## Why this follow-up exists

The historical 95 actually serving senator original-site ledger has independently verified **92/95** people with any authenticated historical owned-site issue excerpts and **1,285** selected short excerpts. All **49/49** sitting 2026 November nominees have at least one such historical excerpt; combined selected historical excerpts **716**. A prior successfully [protected Neon read-only V5 aggregate](source-proof/senate-864-v5-protected-neon-seven-family-baseline.json) observed **140** campaign evidence rows and **65** distinct source documents in the stored `2026` publication-year bucket. This was a count only, with no individual URL, speaker or raw HTML hash join, and did not independently establish the deployed production app's active connection.

**Unit of comparison** is the selected published source URL, not the candidate's entire policy universe.
