The source observer stores whole CDX response SHA256, HTTP code, number of qualifying exact in-domain HTML rows and bounded-index cutoff. At most **four distinct source paths per domain** are re-fetched from the archive at their original UTC capture `id_` timestamp; original *full HTML* SHA-256 is computed before discarding the bytes, and whole original HTML SHA1/Base32 must independently match its actual original CDX `digest` identity. The verified original HTML must explicitly contain the actual serving senator surname.

The metadata-only output contains only original archive source URLs, SHA-256 and CDX digest, archive capture UTC, source status and **nonquotation policy topic cues**, not original HTML, donor names, candidate contact details, addresses, article bodies, raw financial rows or user-personal data. It retains **HOLD** for nonresponsive archive indexes, malformed sources, no original pages, origin/domain mismatches or digest failure. A CDX limit of 200, a failed replay or a new modern website **does not establish the senator lacked positions**.

**No new candidate policy excerpt, individually verified authored timestamp, accepted election-day predictive feature, source-to-database row or 2022 campaign-site completeness claim is produced by this pilot.** Exact original archived words, committee paid-for source attribution and period-appropriate election-cycle context require separate source-review; original archive capture UTC is a public *capture-by* witness, not a first-authored timestamp. 2021–25 separately serving persons and older legislative districts are not automatically the same election platform.
