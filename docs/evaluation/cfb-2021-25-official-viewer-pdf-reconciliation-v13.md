## Historical release and financial-content certification are separate

Under [Minnesota Statutes §10A.20, subdivision 1b](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2024/cite/10A.20), an ordinary report remains nonpublic until **8:00 a.m. the day after it was due**; the report's Board-received date must also be respected (especially for late reports and later amendments). Its source-proven calendar `dueOn` and `boardReceivedOn` define a **not-before** restriction, never an actual contemporaneous public-by. All 229 panel versions still have **UNKNOWN exact historical public-by**. An independently fetched 2026 viewer response validates exact identity *now* but cannot retroactively place a PDF at a historical legislative prediction cutoff. Legal release timing may have different period-specific exceptions; do not apply this ordinary-report floor to a different report category without checking its governing law.

**Financial report reconciliation has not been certified at transaction level in this V13.** The original report-body SHA and full binary identity were already authenticated for 223 distinct PDFs by the V12 source work, but summary totals, in-kind amounts, loans, refunds, transfers, report-specific itemized rows, and original-versus-amended financial changes are not independently re-extracted/compared here. The source-pinned older archived candidate CSV investigations and protected read-only finance metadata pilots do not establish every line's containment in its exact version. No inference about donors, employers or financial influence is made.
