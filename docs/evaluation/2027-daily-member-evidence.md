# Bounded daily member-primary evidence intake (2027)

Follow the incoming-member preseason evidence work (#1618) with a live-session stage of the same single guarded GitHub/Neon collection worker. It rotates up to 12 active members per batch, prioritizing never-attempted incoming members before other never-attempted members, then oldest attempt. No separate scheduler or historical crawl. The existing archive-first GET wrapper admits narrowly scoped official Minnesota House member news archives and two Senate caucus publishers, with up to two directories, 12 member pages and 24 linked article pages (38 requests). The combined ceiling becomes 208 requests while the previous 32 MiB aggregate and eight-minute network deadline remain unchanged. These are pilot caps, not a daily coverage claim or approved live budget.

House archives are exact LRL-ID routes and dated official index links. Senate profiles must have unique directory linkage, full-name and district verification; no guessed profile URL. Articles require the existing member-primary attribution checks plus full-name match, a credible publication date from the article or exact dated House index, current-session scope and original acquisition-time proof. Store original source entity bytes before parsing, verify hashes and source/discovery receipts, and stage immutable candidate manifests containing the unchanged deterministic general issue-position and explicit bill-statement extractors' results. Keep general positions as context, not inferred bill votes. Preserve exact bill and membership identity. Candidate manifests disable serving eligibility, mechanical action and model weight; no inserts into evidence_items or evidence_relationships, and the collector role explicitly refuses their mutation privileges. All raw bodies remain in the private production evidence store if activated later; no full public article bodies in job logs or artifacts.

A separate immutable member-attempt cursor distinguishes failure, no linked recent articles and successful bounded discovery. A successful batch does not prove that all members' news, testimony, interviews, committees or hearings were observed that day. The initial stage is targeted member-primary evidence only. Next verify actual 2027 member sites and article shapes, extend existing committee/hearing and bill-author intake, protect a dedicated evidence reviewer/promoter role and exercise the existing frozen admission tests before canonical ingestion. No model changes, Vercel, credentials, DB calls or schedule activation in this PR. This PR is stacked on the official vote-intake PR #1617; independently review and merge that dependency first.
