## Independent original legislative sources

The [Minnesota Senate Journal, 29th legislative day, Thursday May 1, 2025](https://www.senate.mn/journals/2025-2026/20250501029.pdf) records **Senator Steve Drazkowski moving amendment A-4 to H.F. 2438** (S.F. 2082 companion). The chamber's separate [original A-4 amendment page](https://www.senate.mn/chamber/amendment/sch2438a-4.html) confirms the proposed text concerning *changeable traffic-safety roadway signs*, including a prohibition on witticisms, portmanteaus, slang and pop-culture references. The Senate Journal **printed pages 4354–4357, PDF zero-index pages 26–28** contain the motion, proposal and roll call. These were inspected directly, including the page image.

The official vote was **31 yeas, 34 nays**; the journal states **the amendment was not adopted**. Among the specifically listed senators, **Drazkowski voted yea** and **Ann Johnson Stewart voted nay** (journal roll prints the surnames `Drazkowski` and `Johnson Stewart`). These are **official chamber vote observations on May 1**, not quotes to any reporter. The Journal also lists both as serving senators that day. This official record is an independent date-and-identity cross-check, not proof the live newsroom text matches archived source bytes or was publicly accessible before any model forecast.
