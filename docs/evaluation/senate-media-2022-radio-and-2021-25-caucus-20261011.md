## Evidence gate and next research

An indexed Wayback original must match **exact original URL, publisher HTTP 200 HTML, CDX indexed SHA1 base32, independently verified raw original SHA256 and body size**, and its UTC is an earliest *proven observed* content-present bound—not proof that the article was originally authored or transcribed on its publisher-displayed date. Same calendar day is not necessarily before a vote cutoff. Current MPR page audio URLs are metadata, not original audio. None of the statements is a certified complete recorded voiced quotation, actual Minnesota Senate roll-call, human-approved prediction stance, or confirmed missing/private source document.
