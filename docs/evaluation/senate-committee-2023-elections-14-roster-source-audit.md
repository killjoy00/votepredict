# Issue #864 — 2023 Elections committee 14-member public roster cross-check

The previous nine-source corrected audit, [run #38084229868](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/actions/runs/38084229868), produced **ten** explicit named-roll candidate blocks in **seven** original 2023 Senate Elections Minutes PDFs. These have **133** named YEA/NAY source tokens and **seven** source entries that are clearly Absent or Pass, for **140 total status entries** across ten roll blocks. Every candidate has a 14-person source row set, independent YEA/NAY source numeric parity, and unique source-member names within the block.

The original surname roster used here is **not invented or inferred from party affiliation**: the public 2023 Senate [February 7 Elections hearing minutes](https://www.senate.mn/schedule/hearing_minutes.html?hearing_id=17523&ls=93) list all fourteen committee members present, and the [February 14 minutes](https://www.senate.mn/schedule/hearing_minutes.html?hearing_id=17600&ls=93) list thirteen present and Senator Bruce Anderson absent. The surname set, repeated in both contemporaneous Senate publications, includes Anderson, Bahr, Boldon, Carlson, Cwodzinski, Dornink, Koran, Limmer, Marty, Mathews, Mitchell, Port, Rest and Westlin.

The standalone source-proof module [2023 Elections roster check](../../src/evidence/senate-committee-2023-elections-roll-roster-audit.ts) and [one-time workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-committee-2023-elections-roll-roster.yml) read ONLY the independently SHA-verified **original #38084229868 source audit artifact**, not raw PDFs or senator names copied out of those PDFs. For each of the ten candidate blocks, the module compares the 14 member-side SHA-256 choice fingerprints to each of the fourteen publicly verified committee surnames under exactly four allowed statuses: YEA, NAY, Absent and Pass. It requires every roster surname to appear exactly **once per candidate roll**. A duplicate person on both YEA and NAY, an unidentified surname, omitted member, or a tally mismatched to the source fails closed. For February 7 it separately checks the complete original vote source lists no nonvoting status; for February 14 it requires the single nonvoting member to be **Anderson marked Absent**, not inferred Nay or Yea, matching both the minutes' date-specific attendance header and the source roll.

The goal is narrow: validate machine-read **source surname consistency** against the contemporaneous public committee roster, not independently prove each senator was on the committee or present for every other hearing date. A name matching a roster can still be misattributed within the original source, and the source often contains separate amendment-vote motions with the same tally. February 7's A36/Oral amendment label is ambiguous; no automatic exact motion, floor passage or policy stance may be inferred. February 14 has **two distinct named bill motions**, not one. The 2024 Rules amendment and Ethical Conduct procedural roll sources are **not in scope** of this 2023 Elections roster check.

No full source PDF or original member choice list is archived in this metadata audit, no online account, playback, private production database, 2027 pipeline, forecasting/model/serving/scheduler changed, and no government office was contacted. This does **not** establish 2021 print or 2022 print/electronic Senate committee completeness, nor reconcile any persisted VotePredict member-vote rows. **Keep #864 OPEN.**
