## What this proves — and what is still held

A **historically existing March 24 Hawj written transportation-equity policy statement** predates two documented **May 2024 Senate chamber votes on a different broad omnibus HF 5247**, which *could* be researched as a later policy-to-omnibus event. The present checks prove neither that specific Hawj-named SF language was in the version voted upon, nor that Hawj's YES vote appears on the **original official Senate Journal**, nor that a vote in a multi-policy omnibus is a vote *for each provision*. It is **not** a sound automatic supervised vote prediction label. The final fourth-engrossment source was unavailable to this probe; this is a real independent original-bill-text verification hold.

**Required before any source-to-roll-call/prediction promotion:** independently inspect the exact May 7 / May 19 official Senate Journal individual roll-call entries, confirm Hawj member-specific marks and precise roll call measures (third-reading versus conference); pin actual pre-vote bill-text version showing the relevant standalone language; adjudicate multi-issue bill polarity versus Hawj's quoted policy position; establish historical as-of availability at prediction cutoff; separately reconcile VotePredict source corpus only with explicit authorized restricted export. None of that happened in this PR.
