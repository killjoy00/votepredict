# #864 — nine newly discovered 2023–24 original committee roll-call sources

The full original 2022–25 electronic Minnesota Senate committee PDF audit found 141 original readable Minutes PDFs with a literal roll-call phrase but no recognized named/count-only vote observation. The 41 documents with AYE/NAY *labels* received a separately source-hashed review. Source run [#38081471731](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/actions/runs/38081471731) then independently SHA-reverified the other **100/100** original PDFs lacking AYE/NAY-style *label* formatting and found **nine** with probable numeric vote/division wording, **58** with motion-related context and **33** ambiguous. None were automatically classified as actual votes. All nine stronger cases were clustered in **seven Elections originals from 2023** and **two 2024 Rules-related originals**.

The original excerpts in these nine contain at least three distinct recorded source layouts:
- 2023 Elections: contiguous *Senator Name — Yea/Nay/Absent/Pass* rows after a roll-call request with numeric vote result, sometimes with the numeric source tally in reversed NAYS then YEAS order. **Pass and Absent do not become individual yea or nay.** A missing senator or broken named count remains uncertain.
- 2024 Rules and Administration, February 20: explicit **AYES and NAYS** columns listing senators by surname and first name, followed by a recorded **4 ayes, 6 nays** result on Senator Johnson's A2 amendment to Committee Resolution CR008. This is a committee rule amendment, **not a Senate floor passage vote or bill-position vote**.
- 2024 Rules and Administration Subcommittee on Ethical Conduct, May 7: original official minutes show Senators Champion, Kunesh, Mathews, and Miller each recorded **Nay** on a motion about an ethics complaint involving Senator Nicole Mitchell, with **0 Ayes, 4 Nays** and motion failed. This is **an ethics/complaint procedural action**, not a general policy or floor-vote stance. [Official original PDF](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2024/rulesethical/20240507/rulesethical_20240507_Minutes.pdf).

The [standalone source-only parser](../../src/evidence/senate-committee-nine-no-label-counted-rolls.ts) and [bounded one-time two-year original-PDF workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-committee-nine-unparsed-numeric-originals.yml) will reopen *exactly nine independent, SHA-pinned 2023 and 2024 original PDFs* (3 MiB / 30 pages, HTTPS official authority, no redirects), compare original bytes and extracted text SHA-256 against their previous independent original source manifests, and extract **only explicitly marked YEA/NAY**, with any separately marked Pass/Absent retained as *nonvoting*. Each source numeric tally is independently compared to explicit side counts; a contradictory count or duplicate person is a fail-closed **provisional mismatch**, never an inferred missing senator choice.

Commit neither original PDF nor original source text nor raw named choice entries. Store candidate original URL/date, hashes, roll pattern, explicit YEA/NAY count, separately excluded Pass/Absent count, choice identity SHA-256s, and whether the source-numeric tally matches. An integrity match is still not a human-certified distinct motion or membership/identity reconciliation, nor a model-ready bill stance. Tests prevent attendance names or a generic quorum roll from becoming votes. In particular, the 2024 Rules and ethics procedural actions do not become a policy vote.

The source project still lacks 2021 print-only Senate Minutes reconciliation, 2022 print/electronic differences, 141 indexed meetings without linked original Minutes, independently verified content of 138 alternative media player records, a complete official all-vote denominator and an authorized private DB source-to-row reconciliation. No database reads/writes, scheduler, model/forecast/serving/training or 2027 change; no office contact. Keep #864 OPEN.
