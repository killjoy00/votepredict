**Source-only original-source review, NOT Senate vote certification.** This bounded tranche covers the **64** official Minnesota Legislative Reference Library 2023 Senate committee meeting entries with no linked electronic Minutes and a dated official media `mtgid` record. ## Critical source multiplicity — 58 *hearings*, 63 inspected streams

Across the two source runs, exactly **58 hearing pages** have timestamped caption content: **63 previously authenticated timed caption streams** (a single meeting may have more than one original media/caption stream). Do not interpret one stream as a unique parliamentary motion or automatically coalesce separate streams with similar timestamps.

The first 2023 run records four meetings with **two caption streams** each: 2023-02-16 Education Finance (`1047389`), 2023-03-15 Agriculture/Broadband/Rural Development (`1047752`), 2023-03-15 Education Finance (`1047756`), and 2023-03-20 Jobs/Economic Development (`1047791`). ## Next authenticated content work

The [one-time original source workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-2023-64-unlinked-caption-motion-cues.yml) restores **FOUR** independently SHA-pinned original artifact files, rejects replacement by stale or fabricated reports, re-fetches all **64 exact** dated LRL HTML media pages and requires every page to match its original HTML SHA. It re-fetches **only the 63 previously original-body-SHA-authenticated nonempty caption streams**, checks each full original caption-body SHA/byte length and stream timestamp count, then uses the **strict v2** tagged-visible-text parliamentary procedural cue detector. This avoids the earlier false positive on a generic “move to clean energy” sentence or HTML attributes. Its time-localized review windows are category × 30-second grouping, not a count of unique motions. Up to 120 cue windows **per authenticated stream** (with overflow count) are preserved as timestamp, cue category and SHA of nearby original *visible text*, **without the text itself**. The temporary 30-day artifact contains no transcript snippets, speaker/name attribution, person choices, audio/video, or source media bodies.

This is NOT human playback, official signed minutes, individual recorded vote side evidence, final bill support, proof of publication at an as-of forecast cutoff, or the statewide 2021–25 complete committee motion census.
