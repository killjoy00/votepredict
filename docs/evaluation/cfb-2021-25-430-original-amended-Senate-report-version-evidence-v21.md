### Documentary proof and timing

- Original 2021 year-end government calendar deadline is **January 31, 2022**; original 2022 year-end due **January 31, 2023**. Other report types/periods retain report-specific due dates from their earlier independently verified ledger provenance.
- For ordinary report types under Minn. Stat. [§10A.20, subd. 1b](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/10A.20), the stated legal nonpublicity boundary is **8 a.m. the day after the report was due**, rather than automatically the day after receipt. Later receipt dates and whether the exact signed PDF version actually became accessible must be separately considered. The stored release not-before values are **bounds, never exact PDF publication timestamps**; source proof may conservatively choose a later bound.
- **0 / 430** full exact original/amended PDF version **contemporaneous 2021–25 historical publicly observable-by dates** are independently authenticated. A SHA-confirmed 2026 public download is positive evidence the bytes existed and were retrievable in **2026**, not independent proof the same bytes were online during a prior vote window. A legal timestamp or Board receipt cannot substitute for independently dated capture of the **exact content**.
- Amendments are **not** silently swapped over their originals. The chronological replacement relationship is based on the actual official indexed version sequence; the most current financial record and the historically visible earlier report are different questions.
- These files contain **candidate principal committee** original reports; they do **not** turn individual employee contributions into organizational giving, or equate candidate, party, PAC, independent expenditure, and other entities. No financial totals, loans, expenditure rows, contribution transaction details, amendment monetary deltas or independent-expenditure relationships have yet been certified by **this PDF identity audit**.
