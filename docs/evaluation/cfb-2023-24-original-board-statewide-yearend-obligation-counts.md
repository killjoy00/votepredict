# Original 2023–2024 Minnesota CFB Board year-end report expectations and due counts

Issue #864 campaign-finance **public original-source research only**, observed October 11, 2026. This is an *independent contemporaneous regulator memo* check of the statewide year-end reporting population, **not** a denominator for our 22 actual senator principal committees or 71 report-version references.

Two original Minnesota Campaign Finance and Public Disclosure Board Executive Director **Board Operations** memos have been acquired from their complete original meeting-material PDFs (original binary SHA-256, byte size and exact operative program figures in the permanent [source checkpoint](source-proof/cfb-2023-24-original-board-memo-mixed-filer-yearend-count-source-checkpoint.json)).

| Original contemporaneous Board source | Report year covered | Report deadline | Board's combined program scope and counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [January 31, 2024 memo in February 7, 2024 Board materials](https://cfb.mn.gov/pdf/bdinfo/agendas/2024_02_07_materials.pdf) | **2023** | January 31, 2024 | **1,299 year-end reports EXPECTED** from **candidate committees, political party units, political committees and funds** |
| [February 5, 2025 memo in February 13, 2025 Board materials](https://cfb.mn.gov/pdf/bdinfo/agendas/2025_02_13_materials.pdf) | **2024** | January 31, 2025 | **1,320 year-end reports DUE**, **1,212 filed by deadline**, **64 still outstanding as of February 5, 2025**; all the same combined filer categories |

**Do not convert the 2023 expected figure to confirmed legal obligations or filed reports:** the January 31, 2024 memo predates a final reconciliation of 2023 year-end filings. Likewise, 2024's 64 still outstanding as of February 5 **does not mean** 64 were late at January 31; it is a later observation. The 2024 memo's scope includes candidates running for the Minnesota House, statewide offices and others, party units, independent political committees and funds. It does not supply a Senate committee-specific breakout.

**Important difference from past samples:** CFB original candidate viewer PDF references for the 22 independently proven actually 2022-serving senators show 44 original 2023–2024 year-end version IDs (plus amended version indices) and 68 distinct authentic source PDF bodies. The source observations do not prove which committees owed a report under all registration/termination exceptions. These two aggregate contemporaneous Board memos do not repair that missing committee-level duty evidence. Nor do they show exactly when any historical version became publicly readable.
