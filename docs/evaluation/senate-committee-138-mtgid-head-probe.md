# Issue #864 — 138 exact dated official Senate media record IDs, HEAD-only archival metadata

The 141 no-linked-Minutes meeting date sections have yielded exactly **138 distinct official Minnesota Legislative Reference Library media-record URLs** under `https://www.lrl.mn.gov/media/file?mtgid=<id>`: 2022 eleven, 2023 sixty-four, 2024 forty-one, 2025 twenty-two, with three original meetings having no media link. All 138 original record URL SHA-256 values match the previous fully source-hashed exact-date HTML review [#897](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/pull/897), source run #38079233118. These are **individual official archive record IDs**, not generic portal URLs. However a valid record URL is still **not proof of accessible playback, a complete recording, a heard motion, or named voting choices**.

The one-time [metadata-only workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-committee-138-mtgid-head.yml) acquires only the pinned original four-year source artifact JSONs and performs **HTTP HEAD** (no GET media, no redirects) against precisely these 138 original LRL URLs, with 2 concurrent official requests per year. It records the official HTTP status, content type/length, and for redirects only source category, hostname and path digest. It cannot see or download the audio/video body, does not accept redirects as successfully played recordings, and does not make transcript or human speaker assertions. A HEAD 405, 403, 404, 410 or failed response is an unresolved accessibility state, not 'no recorded vote'. A 200 is **only** evidence of a response to an archive metadata request, not certified playback. All 138 source URLs are validated to be exact official unique `mtgid` IDs, never user-controlled arbitrary targets.

The LRL media archive indexes Senate audio since 1991 and video since 2001 but notes that not every meeting has both. The separate Granicus Senate archive warns about potential deletion of some 2016–2022 video by December 31, 2026; the urgency is to **preserve verified public metadata**, not to bulk-download content without an approved acquisition plan or contact government offices. 2021 Senate print-only Minutes and the 2022 print/electronic mismatch remain open. No historical private database, serving/model/forecast/training or scheduler changes, and no 2027 work. Keep #864 open.
