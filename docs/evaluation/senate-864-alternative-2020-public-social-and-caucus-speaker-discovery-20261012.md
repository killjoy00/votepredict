# #864 — Is the historical candidate website source track exhausted? Alternative public statement evidence research

**Discovery and strict non-admission checkpoint — October 12, 2026.**

This is a bounded follow-on to [merged original archive PR #1250](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/pull/1250), after three 2013–17 Common Crawl lanes produced **12 actual HTTP 504** archive-index failures, not any authenticated original candidate-owned website page. This checkpoint **does not** itself authenticate additional original authored 2020 posts or make a candidate's own pre-election policy statement claim.

## Current baseline is unchanged

- **95** actual-serving Minnesota state senators during 2021–2025, independently reconciled to the original LRL government service records.
- **92/95** have *at least one* authenticated original candidate-owned historical website excerpt, not necessarily a complete or same-cycle campaign platform.
- **1,285** selected original candidate-owned short campaign-site excerpts, not an exhaustive count of positions.
- **Wiger 2020 SD43; Tomassoni 2020 SD6; Bakk 2020 SD3** remain without an authenticated original candidate-owned *Senate* website passage. **None is classified as having no positions or no campaign site.**
- The original 2020 and 2022 Minnesota SOS 21-column *filed-candidate* exports with campaign website in field 16 are still missing. Historical SOS names on Senate-only pre-election lists and the post-general seven-column `cand.txt` cannot establish the missing website field.
- Publisher direct Q&As, caucus authored statements, and legislators' public social posts are **separate provenance categories**. They cannot increment 92/95 or 1,285.

## Route actually explored: University-hosted historical social communications

[CivicWatch, University of Pittsburgh](https://picso101.sci.pitt.edu/CivicWatch/about) offers individual state-legislator public activity pages drawn from a research corpus with data through December 2024.

Two original 2020 winner/actually-serving-person matches present **concrete independent third-party statement leads**, not candidate-owned campaign website text:

| MN senator | Public account displayed by research interface | Research interface count, including reposts | Displayed period | Example *topic* of displayed historically dated first-person text |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Chuck Wiger, 2020 SD43** | [@ChuckWiger](https://picso101.sci.pitt.edu/CivicWatch/who/3290230800) | **2,093 posts** | Jan 9, 2020–Dec 6, 2022 | Sept 11, 2020 remarks concerning prior preservation legislation; note other prominent entries are *reposts*, not Wiger's original words |
| **David Tomassoni, 2020 SD6** | [@Ranuccio11](https://picso101.sci.pitt.edu/CivicWatch/who/3189770262) | **96 posts** | Jan 19, 2020–Dec 11, 2021 | Jan 19, 2020 PolyMet appeals and employment comment; Oct 16, 2020 Line 3 investment and employment comment |

These are **reported corpus post totals** and must not be called 2,189 unique, candidate-authored policy positions. Original IDs, original platform text or complete archived post bodies, exact original source public-by timestamps, and exact duplicate comparisons have **not** been recovered in this round. For instance, a Wiger repost of news reported by NPR is **not his verbatim Senate policy statement**.

**Party chronology caution:** the present CivicWatch Tomassoni profile retrospectively labels his whole profile **Independent**. Original SOS **November 3, 2020 SD6 election winner** evidence identifies his election party as **DFL**. Do not assign a post's source-year party from the current profile label. Likewise, the interface's modeled topic and derived voting-record position are *research metadata*, not evidence that he personally endorsed every word of a retweet or how he voted on a Senate motion.

The academic dataset route is real: [Tai et al., *Scientific Data*, September 25, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05857-1) documents the Digitally Accountable Public Representation Database (DAPR), whose X/Twitter collection started in 2020 and whose public **weekly aggregates include lists of original post URLs**. Its authors describe a collection procedure using original platform APIs beginning in 2020 and resuming under changed terms in 2024. The freely published aggregate files are not a public full-text/post-level original archive. The [Harvard Dataverse landing](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/A9EPYJ) required a JavaScript bot verification in this research environment; this checkpoint **did not** bypass it or claim downloaded post IDs. It also does **not yet prove that the two specific CivicWatch profile entries are exact records in the public DAPR dataset**. The projects are related by stated provenance, not a verified per-post join.

A valid next self-service test would obtain **specific original platform status URLs/IDs** through a publicly available original archive or the published dehydrated data, independently verify the account identity, and validate the platform Snowflake ID's embedded creation timestamp against the displayed date. A numeric ID corroborates a timestamp **only**, not the precise historical posted words or who composed them. Exact original body/speaker scope still needs independent proof before admitting any specific quote. Original observation dates and author-first-publication dates must be kept separate.

## Second genuinely distinct route: 2020 first-person caucus-published statements

[**July 20, 2020 — Senator Wiger Statement on Inadequate Support for School Safety**](https://senatedfl.mn/senator-wiger-statement-on-inadequate-support-for-school-safety/) is an available original Minnesota Senate DFL caucus-hosted page today. It displays the senator as the named speaker, a July 20, 2020 publication date and direct school-reopening safety and public-funding commentary. This exact URL/title was not located by the searched repository source-ledger code query. **That is only an unduplicated-source *lead*, not proof the original 2020 body was independently archived or that no existing shorter quote overlaps it.** No new caucus remark was admitted here.

Do not confuse caucus-published individual direct speech with a candidate-owned site or independently certified recorded Senate votes. To admit the original as historical cutoff evidence, recover an independent contemporaneous body/version and dated original source receipt or other acceptable proof under the project standard, then reconcile named short statements against existing Wiger speaker ledgers.

## Lower-yield candidate website options checked

The Minnesota SOS [Candidate Filings](https://www.sos.mn.gov/election-administration-campaigns/data-maps/candidate-filings/) page still points to the current-year filing portal and separates pre-election filing data from post-election results. The current public portal is for **November 3, 2026**, not a historical 2020/2022 website-bearing filing export. Public search yielded no independently authenticated historical 21-column originals; there is **no** basis for reading a 2026 field as historical.

[Scott Newman's 2020 BallotReady profile](https://www.ballotready.org/people/scott-newman-f27b0694-edfa-4fe3-96be-64f5e969497c) still links issue statements to his original campaign site. Earlier PR #1233 independently verified his original 2020 campaign homepage via Common Crawl, but **not** the complete contemporaneous `/issues/` HTML. The presently republished BallotReady summaries may guide source discovery but cannot substitute for unverified original-owned historical text.

[Tom Bakk's dated October 8, 2020 KAXE Senate District 3 interview](https://www.kaxe.org/vote-2020/2020-10-08/meet-senator-tom-bakk-dfl-candidate-for-senate-3-in-lake-cook-st-louis-koochiching-counties) is **already** in authenticated speaker-scoped publisher research ledgers. It is useful existing public material, not a new owned website or new nonduplicated statement.

The [iVoterGuide 2020 Wiger candidate profile](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/50436/race/12295/election/801) shows repeated **“Did not answer”** next to its questions. Those are questions from the publisher, **not Wiger's endorsements, denials, positions or a complete proof that he spoke nowhere else**.

## Practical stop / continue decision

**Stop this activity:** repeated 2020/2022 SOS postback or CAPTCHA attempts, repeated Wayback/CDX 504s and same Common Crawl unavailable collections, generic campaign domain guessing for the final three, and attempted elevation of old Bakk Governor pages/repurposed Tomassoni pages into Senate-site evidence.

**Continue in a separate speaker-attributed category only if a concrete self-service original record can be verified:** original social status ID plus contemporaneous provenance and person-specific text, or an independently dated original Senate caucus/publisher statement page not already audited. Start with a **small 2020 Wiger/Tomassoni pilot** rather than automatically harvesting thousands of research mirror posts. A later source lead may be useful even without historic prediction eligibility, but any such limitations must remain explicit.

**Reasonable stopping condition:** after checking the source's actual original-ID and dated body availability, if we still cannot authenticate historical public-by or speaker text, keep the bounded research lead and stop probing it. Neither missing archives nor closed access controls may be bypassed or converted into negative statements about candidates.
