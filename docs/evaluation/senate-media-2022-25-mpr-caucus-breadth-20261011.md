## Why evidence wording and member attribution must be narrow

An **original archived publisher page** (even with exact time and SHA) proves only what that page contained **no later than its authenticated capture time**. Publisher-displayed broadcast day, today-served site, PDF embedded creation date and current HTTP `Last-Modified` do not prove transcript first-publication minute. A source may have a different transcript revision and audio publication history. An explicit named speaker text turn is evidence of publisher-printed attribution; **full recorded speech and audio binary equality have not been independently proved**, and no full human-reviewed quote is approved.

Likewise, a named-person **party-caucus individual statement** is stronger potential first-party attribution than a journalist paraphrase, but cannot be retroactively modeled as available before an independently established public-by time. A **joint caucus statement** is not three personal quotations. News about a law after passage (Duckworth's 2024 interview about the 2023 school meals law; SGU veterans joint release after the April 2025 passage) cannot predict those already occurred votes. No `source_documents` or `evidence_items` rows were viewed, and no absence from VotePredict's private corpus was established.
