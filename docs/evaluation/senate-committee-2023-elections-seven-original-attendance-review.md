# Issue #864 — compare actual 2023 Elections meeting attendance to named roll choice source sections

The corrected original-source Senate committee audit [#38084229868](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/actions/runs/38084229868) recovered **ten named YEA/NAY source roll candidate blocks** from seven official 2023 Senate Elections PDFs, with **133 explicitly named YEA/NAY choices and seven separately excluded Absent/Pass source statuses**. A separate verified [public historical fourteen-member roster audit #38085294202](https://github.com/killjoy00/votepredict/actions/runs/38085294202) matched all 140 choice-status fingerprints to the correct contemporary roster. **That roster audit alone does not prove which senators actually attended each hearing.**

The bounded [seven-meeting original-source workflow](../../.github/workflows/senate-committee-seven-2023-attendance.yml) re-fetches only the **seven SHA-pinned original 2023 Elections Minutes PDFs** already independently discovered, preserving the original binary and embedded-text SHA-256 checks, 3 MiB/30-page limits, no redirects, and an immutable [12-motion source-offset queue](./source-proof/senate-committee-nine-tally-matched-source-action-review-queue.json). Every one of the ten source roll candidates is re-extracted, associated by **exact original PDF URL, committee hearing date and text offset**, and compared to the *same original PDF's opening meeting-level Present and Absent roster*. The comparison stores only roster counts, SHA-256 identity fingerprints of affected committee surnames, source original hashes, exact source offsets and resolution/contradiction categories—not original PDF bytes, full text, names or individual choice lists.

Each opening meeting roster must uniquely partition all fourteen historical Elections senators into explicitly Present and Absent before it can be marked an independently complete attendance header. If a name is in that meeting's Absent header but in an explicit YEA or NAY roll choice, flag a **source contradiction requiring human review**. If a name is in the opening Present roster but marked Absent in a later *motion-specific* roll, flag a **possible later departure**, not automatically a contradiction: initial meeting attendance does not establish continuous presence. Missing/ambiguous source headers or printed-date discrepancies stay unverified. The official Minnesota Senate [February 7](https://www.senate.mn/schedule/hearing_minutes.html?hearing_id=17523&ls=93) and [February 14](https://www.senate.mn/schedule/hearing_minutes.html?hearing_id=17600&ls=93) published minutes independently corroborate those two days, but the other five meeting dates still need original source-level attendance validation.

This is not an automatic promotion of candidate individual votes into the production historical database or a comprehensive recorded committee vote census. It does not resolve 2025 Elections meeting-heading/absent-voter contradictions, the 2024 Rules ethics/procedural vote context, the 2021 Senate print-only Minutes, the 2022 print/electronic differences, or 141 committee meetings with no directly linked electronic Minutes PDFs. No production DB, model, forecast, training, serving, scheduler, 2027 changes or government office contact. **Keep #864 OPEN.**

**Call-to-order parser guard:** The originally listed `Absent: Senator Surname` section must not be truncated merely because a different senator subsequently `called the meeting to order`. This boundary now requires a distinct call-to-order line; absent members are never silently classified as present, and ambiguous source layout remains a review-needed result rather than certified attendance.
