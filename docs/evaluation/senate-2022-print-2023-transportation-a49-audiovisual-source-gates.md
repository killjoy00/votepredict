## 2022 April 7 Finance original print vs electronic

The LRL's [2022 Senate committee minute index](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/minutes/default?body=senate&year=2022) expressly warns that separate electronic and print 2022 minute collections may contain different or missing records. The [LRL main minutes collection](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/minutes/) describes the **print originals as physically held** at the Legislative Reference Library, not automatically exposed as the same downloadable PDF. This is a hard remaining source-availability boundary for source-only remote research, **not proof that the source does not exist**.

Previous [seven original 2022 Finance electronic roll paragraph leads](senate-2022-finance-apr07-seven-electronic-rolls-review.md) came from the [published electronic minutes PDF](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2022/Fin/20220407/Fin_20220407_minutes.pdf): 67 explicitly named YEA/NAY tokens across SF3510, SF4269 and SF4091 and three explicitly absent tokens. They are research/source-text proof only, not a comparison with the separate print volume. Source SF4269 A17 has no isolated printed absent field in available text, so **null remains null** and neither an opening attendance roster nor all ten YEA/NAY names may silently replace what the absent field actually printed.

The **2022 print pages were not obtained or visually checked** in this session. No print-original PDF SHA or exact print-versus-electronic source-vote parity should be claimed; no full official denominator is known. No outreach to the Legislature or LRL was made.

## 2023 April 3 Transportation SF3157 oral A49 / misheaded A26

The previously original-HTML-authenticated [Senate meeting minutes](https://www.senate.mn/schedule/hearing_minutes.html?always_show_minutes=Y&hearing_id=17939&ls=93&type=minutes) and the [published official LRL electronic minutes PDF, page 3](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2023/fintrans/20230403/fintrans_20230403_minutes.pdf) both show the following **same** source contradiction: an earlier Jasinski **A26** motion was separately introduced and failed **2 YEA / 6 NAY / 2 Absent**. Subsequently Howe moved an **oral amendment to A49**, describing an insertion on line 1.16, requested a roll on *that oral amendment*, and the printed 2/6/2 result is followed by a conclusion referring to **A26** not adopted. The independent file *formats* do **not** constitute independent contemporaneous speakers: they may have been generated from identical minutes content. The named-side hash proof previously authenticated seven published roll sections, **not original live audio corroboration of A49**.

Primary media discovery is exact: the [Senate Granicus archived-audio publisher](https://mnsenate.granicus.com/ViewPublisher.php?view_id=2) lists **Transportation, April 3, 2023** with an [archived MP3](https://archive-video.granicus.com/mnsenate/mnsenate_05ee7227-6441-421d-834c-8b3a48059cea.mp3). The [Granicus video publisher](https://mnsenate.granicus.com/ViewPublisher.php?view_id=1) lists the April 3 Committee on Transportation recording beginning 2:09 p.m., length approximately 3 hours 44 minutes. **The actual audio/video was not obtained, byte-authenticated, listened to, transcribed or matched to a unique A49 motion timecode**. The archival audio domain was unreachable from the current code environment, and a listed file is not a transcript or verified audible vote. There is no corroborated source basis to decide whether the final A26 wording is a typo or whether a separate A49 motion actually received a counted roll at that point.
