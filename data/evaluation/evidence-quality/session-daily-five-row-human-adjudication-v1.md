## Sponsorship rule added with this adjudication

Verified bill sponsorship, co-sponsorship, authorship, chief authorship, or an unambiguous first-person authorship relation (for example, “my bill” or “I introduced HF123”) is supportive evidence for that exact bill.

Canonical dated Minnesota Revisor authorship is the authoritative structured source where available. Textual sources may corroborate sponsorship, but ambiguous identity or bill linkage fails closed. Merely presenting, discussing, chairing, hearing, moving, or procedurally handling a bill is not sponsorship.

This semantic rule does **not** give sponsorship a model weight or mechanically change a forecast. Existing `billAuthor` model weight remains zero unless separately authorized.

No model fitting, feature freeze, probability change, or serving change is authorized by this record.
